Homeownership Just Got More Accessible

Saving for a down payment is often the biggest hurdle to buying a home. That is why I am thrilled to offer an exclusive financing program designed to help you achieve your real estate goals faster and with less out-of-pocket cash.

Read through the requirements and benefits of the program below to see how we can put this incredible tool to work for you.

$0 Down Payment

This program allows you to purchase a home with absolutely zero dollars required for a down payment, keeping your savings in your pocket where they belong.

$88,000 Income Limit

The program features a strict income limit of $88,000 per year. However, this limit only applies to the individual(s) officially listed on the loan. If two people plan to co-own the home, you can strategically structure it so only the qualifying earner is on the loan.

True Market Interest Rates

Unlike many specialty financing options, this program provides a standard, competitive market interest rate. There are absolutely no hidden markups on the rate or your closing costs.

Powered by Sunflower Bank

This program is a highly specialized lending product made possible through my direct professional partnership with the experts at Sunflower Bank.

No Initial Credit Check

Want to see if this is an option for you? No problem. There is absolutely no hard credit check required to see if your financial scenario qualifies for the program.
