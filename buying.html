The Buying Process

A clear, step-by-step guide to securing your dream home with confidence and strategic precision.

Your Path to Homeownership

Buying a house is one of the most exciting financial milestones of your life. However, navigating the Arizona real estate market requires an organized strategy and an advocate in your corner.

Below is a roadmap of what you can expect when we partner together to find your perfect property. From our very first conversation to the moment I hand you the keys, I am here to ensure the process is seamless and successful.

Step 01

Initial Information

To get started, simply fill out my brief buyer intake form. This provides me with the essential initial information regarding your budget, timeline, and the specific criteria you are looking for in a home.

Fill Out the Form
Step 02

Refine The Best Strategy

Once I have your details, we will connect to refine your strategy. We will evaluate your buying power, connect you with trusted lenders, and decide exactly what loan product will work best for your unique situation.

Step 03

Set Up The Collection

I will curate a custom, real-time collection of homes on the market that strictly match the criteria you are looking for. You will be able to review, favorite, and reject properties in one streamlined portal.

Step 04

Tour Homes

This is the fun part! Once you have identified your top choices from the collection, we will schedule private showings and hit the pavement to tour the homes you are most interested in.

Step 05

Offer

When we find "the one," we will sit down and discuss the best way to structure a competitive offer. I will use deep market data to negotiate fiercely on your behalf and get you under contract.

Step 06

Escrow

Once the seller accepts your offer, we enter escrow. I will guide you through this secure holding phase, coordinating property inspections, handling the appraisal, and ensuring your final loan approval goes smoothly.

Step 07

Get Keys

Closing day! After signing your final paperwork and the loan funding clears, we officially record the sale. I will hand over the keys, and you can celebrate becoming a new homeowner!

Ready to Take the First Step?

Before we can dive into the collection and start touring, we have to start with Step 1. Filling out the initial intake form is completely zero-pressure. In fact, it is a great idea to do this even if you are months or years away from buying—it gives you a crystal-clear picture of your options and what you are able to do right now.
