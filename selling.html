The Selling Process

A clear, step-by-step guide to maximizing your home's value and selling with confidence.

Your Path to a Successful Sale

Selling your house is a major financial milestone. Navigating the Arizona real estate market requires an organized strategy, aggressive marketing, and a relentless advocate in your corner.

Below is a roadmap of what you can expect when we partner together to list your property. From our very first conversation to the moment your home officially closes, I am here to ensure the process is seamless, profitable, and entirely stress-free.

Step 01

Initial Information

To get started, simply fill out my brief intake form. This provides me with the essential initial information regarding your property, timeline, and unique goals.

Step 02

Refine Strategy

Once I have your details, we will connect to refine your selling strategy. We will evaluate the current market, review a customized Comparative Market Analysis (CMA), and decide on the absolute best pricing strategy for your home.

Step 03

Prepare the Home

First impressions are everything. We will work together to clean up the property if needed, professionally stage it to highlight its best features, and capture high-end listing photos to make it shine online.

Step 04

Launch

Your home goes live! The opening weekend will always feature a strategic open house to maximize exposure. I will provide prompt, transparent communication regarding any private showings, feedback, and incoming offers.

Step 05

Negotiate

Every offer is unique, and price isn't the only factor. We will review all terms together and discuss what the best, most advantageous counter-offer might consist of to ensure your interests are protected.

Step 06

Escrow

Once we accept an offer, we enter escrow. I will skillfully help you navigate the buyer's inspection and any repair negotiations, as well as guide you through the necessary legal paperwork required to close.

Step 07

Sold

Congratulations, your home is officially sold! After the final paperwork is signed and the deed is recorded, you can celebrate your successful sale. And don't worry—I am never afraid to roll up my sleeves and help you move!
